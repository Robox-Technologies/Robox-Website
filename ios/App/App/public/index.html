About us

Build it. Code it. Bring it to life.

Ro/Box is an ultra-affordable robotics kit designed to make STEM education accessible to any student, anywhere. Whether you’re a budding engineer or a curious coder, Ro/Box offers hands-on experience in building, programming, and problem-solving

Box-to-Bot in 4 Steps

We know how difficult robotics can be.
Its why we've made Ro/Box easy to follow for all ages.

1

Unbox Your Kit

Open your Ro/Box kit and take out all the components. Make sure you have everything you need before starting.

2

Assemble the Robot

Follow the step-by-step instructions to assemble your robot. Take your time and ensure all parts are securely connected.

3

Connect to the App

Download the Ro/Box app on your device and connect it to your robot via Bluetooth. This will allow you to program and control your robot.

4

Start Coding

Use the app's coding interface to start programming your robot. Experiment with different commands and watch your robot come to life!
